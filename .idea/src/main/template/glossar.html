Neo racism Alternative forms neo-racism Etymology neo- +‎ racism (ideology of different treatment for different races): 21st century. Noun neoracism (countable and uncountable, plural neoracisms) (uncountable) Discrimination or prejudice based on nationality and ethnicity or cultural differences. (countable) An instance of neoracism, or a type of neoracism that targets a particular nationality. (uncountable, derogatory) An ideology that rejects the idea of color blindness and believes in different treatment for different races. wiktionary
